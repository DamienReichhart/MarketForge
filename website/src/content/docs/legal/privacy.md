*Last updated: 16 November 2026.*

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit this website, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French *Loi Informatique et Libertés*. The short version: **this site has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no accounts and no forms.** The only personal data involved is what any web server sees when it delivers a page, and that is handled by the host.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Damien REICHHART**, the publisher of this site (see the [legal notice](../notice/)). Contact: [contact@damien-reichhart.fr](mailto:contact@damien-reichhart.fr).

## What this site does not do

- No analytics or audience measurement, first- or third-party.
- No advertising, tracking pixels, social-media widgets or embedded third-party content.
- No cookies (see the [cookie policy](../cookies/)).
- No external requests: fonts, scripts, styles and images are all served by this site itself. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts, a CDN or any other third party while you read it.
- Search runs entirely in your browser. The search index is a set of files downloaded from this site; what you type is never sent anywhere.

## What is processed, and why

### Server logs kept by the host

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub receives the technical data your browser sends with each request: your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser’s user agent. GitHub states that it logs the IP address of GitHub Pages visitors **for security purposes**, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.

- **Purpose:** delivering the pages and protecting the service against abuse.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in publishing a website that works and stays secure.
- **Access:** the publisher does not receive these logs and has no access to them.
- **Retention and transfers:** set by GitHub. GitHub, Inc. is located in the United States and transfers data under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Emails you send

If you write to the contact address, your email address and message are used only to answer you and to handle any request you make (for example, exercising your rights). **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in answering, or a legal obligation when you exercise a right. They are deleted once the exchange is over, at the latest three years after your last message.

### Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, PyPI and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you follow the link.

## The MarketForge software

MarketForge itself runs entirely on your machine. It makes no network connection, collects no telemetry and sends no data to the publisher or anyone else. Installing it from PyPI is governed by the [PyPI privacy notice](https://policies.python.org/pypi.org/Privacy-Notice/).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to [contact@damien-reichhart.fr](mailto:contact@damien-reichhart.fr); you will get an answer within one month. For data held by GitHub in its server logs, the publisher will forward your request, and you can also contact GitHub directly through its [privacy contact form](https://support.github.com/contact/privacy).

You may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France; [cnil.fr/fr/plaintes](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes), in French), or with the authority of the EU country where you live.

## Changes

If this site ever starts processing more data (for example, by adding audience measurement), this page will be updated **before** that happens, and the date at the top will change.
