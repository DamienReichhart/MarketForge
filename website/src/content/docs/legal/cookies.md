*Last updated: 16 November 2026.*

**This website sets no cookies**, its own or anyone else’s, which is why it shows no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. It has no analytics, no advertising and no embedded third-party content.

## What is stored in your browser

Two small values are stored by your browser, never sent to any server, and used only to make the site work the way you asked:

| Name | Where | What it holds | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `starlight-theme` | Local storage | Your choice of light, dark or automatic theme, if you changed it. | Until you clear your browser’s site data. |
| `sl-sidebar-state` | Session storage | Which documentation sidebar groups are open, so they stay open from one page to the next. | Until you close the tab. |

Under Article 82 of the French *Loi Informatique et Libertés* (which implements Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive), storage that is strictly necessary for a service you explicitly asked for is exempt from consent. Both values fall under that exemption: they contain nothing about you beyond the choices you made on this page, are never read by anyone but your own browser, and the site works without them.

To remove them, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or browse in a private window.

## The host

The host, GitHub Pages, does not set cookies on this site. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for the server logs it keeps.

## Changes

If cookies or any other tracker are ever added, this page will be updated, and consent will be asked for **before** anything that requires it is stored.
